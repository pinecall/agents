# Handing a call to a person

This page moved to [docs.pinecall.io](https://docs.pinecall.io), where it is written as short pages,
one idea each, checked against the code:

- [Handing a call to a person](https://docs.pinecall.io/guides/handing-off/) — Transfer the caller, or ask your team to take the line.
- [Supervising a live call](https://docs.pinecall.io/guides/supervising/) — Listen, whisper to the agent, take the line and hand it back.
- [Callbacks](https://docs.pinecall.io/guides/callbacks/) — A caller the agent promised a call back, waiting for your team.
